# Asset licenses — Eccentri City

This document tracks provenance for 3D/content assets. Godot (MIT) does not
dictate content licenses; each asset must be cleared separately.

## Project code

Unless noted otherwise, project scripts under `scripts/` and tools under
`tools/` (except vendored third-party downloads) are part of this repository.

### MakeHuman Community clothing packs

The downloader copies each pack's per-asset metadata (author, license, source URL) out of the
pack zip into `assets/humans/outfits/licenses/<pack>.json`. Those files are the authoritative
record for every community asset that reaches an outfit GLB.

**`_cc0` in the pack filename is not a guarantee.** Two packs ship an item whose author
relicensed it to CC-BY years after the pack zip was built, so the license is read per asset from
the pack metadata and anything that is not CC0 is refused before installation:

| Asset | Pack | Actual license | Action |
|-------|------|----------------|--------|
| `culturalibre_heroine_boots_4` | `shoes01_cc0.zip` | CC-BY | refused, not installed |
| `culturalibre_hero_mask_5` | `masks01_cc0.zip` | CC-BY | refused, not installed |

Several community items are labelled CC0 while being fan art of trademarked characters. The
label does not make them safe to redistribute, so the downloader refuses those by name too
(`TRADEMARK_BLOCKLIST` in `tools/download_mh_clothes_packs.py`):

| Asset | Pack | Reason |
|-------|------|--------|
| `green_tomato_rei_ayanami` | `suits02_cc0.zip` | fan art of a trademarked character |
| `slayer227_spider-gwen` | `suits02_cc0.zip` | fan art of a trademarked character |

## Creature bodies (undead and monsters)

| Asset | Source | License |
|-------|--------|---------|
| `assets/monsters/kaykit_skeletons/**` | [KayKit Character Pack: Skeletons](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-skeletons) by Kay Lousberg | **CC0** |
| `assets/monsters/quaternius_monsters/**` | [Quaternius Ultimate Monsters](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html), the author's own Google Drive folder `18m4KpzpEzhC9wl7jzr6dUc0N8Jozr79C` | **CC0** |

`assets/monsters/quaternius_monsters/License.txt` is the licence file bundled with the
download, vendored unedited. It is the author's boilerplate and names a different pack of
his ("Ultimate Platformer Pack") while stating CC0 1.0 Universal; the pack page states the
same terms. **Sketchfab and scraper mirrors of Ultimate Monsters sometimes claim CC-BY.**
They are not the source this repository used and not the terms it relies on.

Fetch and conversion: `python tools/fetch_quaternius_monsters.py`. The pack ships
self-contained `.gltf` with the 9 KB `Atlas_Monsters.png` base64'd into every one of the
fifty files; the script rewrites each as `.glb` pointing at a single shared copy of the
atlas, 30.6 MiB of `.gltf` down to 16.4 MiB of `.glb`. Geometry, rigs and animation clips
are untouched.

All fifty Quaternius models are vendored. Which of them the game may actually spawn is a
separate question answered by `scripts/city/creature_catalog.gd`, not by this file: the
seventeen `flying/` bodies are in no spawn table because navigation here is ground spans
with no flight system, and `blob/Ninja` because its rest pose stands below y=0.
